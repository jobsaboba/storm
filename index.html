The picks

Sandstorm's locked rule v5, unchanged:

  • UK & Irish all-weather flat handicaps.
  • Course & distance winner — Sporting Life's own career C+D stat.
  • Ran within the last 28 days.
  • Today's official rating strictly below the last visible C+D winning mark. If the winning run isn't visible, the horse is allowed.
  • Priced 3/1–33/1 at selection.
  • Ranked shortest odds first, freshest last run as tiebreak; at most three per race.

Every qualifier is eligible. The 8+ runner rule belongs to the each-way book; a win-only bet does not need place terms.

The money

  • Bank starts at £10.00.
  • Each horse carries 1% of the bank at the start of its race, rounded to the penny.
  • Win: profit = stake × SP.
  • Any other finish: lose the stake. There is no place return.
  • All picks in one race use the same pre-race bank; the bank moves after the race.
  • Non-runners, withdrawn and abandoned/void races are no bets. Non-finishers are losses. Dead heats share the stake correctly.
  • If the bank falls below 50p, 1% rounds below 1p and the book stops betting.
This is a win-only book. There is no each-way or place component. A horse finishing second or third loses the full stake.

The data

  • Picks come from Sandstorm's stored drafts-28 selection sheet.
  • Settlement uses the actual starting price / final odds shown on Sporting Life's result pages.
  • The daily refresh settles races as they finish and pushes the updated bank.
  • Historical picks are the stored Sandstorm backtest sheet. Because that sweep was fetched after racing, historical pick odds often already equal the SP; live picks are scored in the morning and settle at the actual SP later.

Win-only removes the place returns that carried the each-way book. The result is a much harsher bankroll, and the season page shows exactly how it played out.

Source: sportinglife.com racecards & results. This is a backtest, not a guarantee.

Reading the card

  • Pick is the price Sandstorm selected at. SP is the actual starting price used to settle.
  • WIN pays the SP. LOSE is any other finish, including second and third.
  • Placed (unpaid) marks a horse that finished in the places but gets no money in this book.
  • NO BET means the bank can't fund a 1p stake, or there is no runnable selection.
  • VOID is an abandoned/void race; NR is a non-runner. Neither changes the bank.
  • PENDING is a race that hasn't resulted yet; the target 1% stake is shown but not deducted.
